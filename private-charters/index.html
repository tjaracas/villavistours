Good to know

A few quick answers.

How many guests can join?

The charter is planned for groups of up to 30 guests. The final number is confirmed when your booking is discussed.

How long is the trip?

Charter duration is arranged with you. Tell us how much time you have and what kind of outing you want.

Can we arrange drinks, food or sushi?

Yes, optional drinks, snacks and catering can be discussed when you request your quote. These extras are confirmed in advance.

Where do we depart?

Departure details will be shared with your booking confirmation.

What does it cost?

Every group and occasion can be different. Send us your date and group size and we'll provide a clear quote before you confirm.
